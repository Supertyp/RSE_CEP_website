# Problem Space — RSE CEP Website

## The project

The Research Software Engineering Capacity Enhancement Project (RSE CEP) is an ANU initiative building research software engineering capacity through training, community development, and knowledge sharing. The project needs a professional public website to communicate its work, share outputs, and engage with the research community.

## The problem

The site launched as a functional skeleton: five pages (Home, Team, Blog, Contact, Search), four blog posts, and Pagefind search. It works, but launched with a generic visual identity (system fonts, a stock blue accent), no mobile navigation, and no search link in the primary nav. As the first public face of the project, it needs to clearly signal institutional affiliation and be properly usable across devices.

The first design effort focuses on the visual layer: applying ANU brand identity, establishing a consistent typography system, enabling dark mode, and making the site properly usable on mobile.

## Who has the problem

The six-person RSE CEP team at ANU, who need a professional public presence that reflects the project's institutional context. Researchers and institutions discovering the project form first impressions from the site's visual quality and usability.

## Current alternatives

None — this is the only dedicated public presence for the project.

## What good looks like

The site is visually consistent with ANU identity, loads a professional typeface from self-hosted assets, adapts cleanly to mobile, supports dark mode via OS preference, and makes search reachable from the primary navigation.
