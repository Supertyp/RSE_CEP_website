We are pleased to launch the official website of the **Research Software Engineering Capacity Enhancement Project (RSE CEP)**.

This site will serve as the primary hub for project communications, publications, and updates. Here you will find:

- **Project news and announcements** via this blog
- **Published outputs** — reports, tools, and resources produced by the project
- **Team information** — who we are and how to get in touch

We will post regularly as the project develops. Stay tuned.
